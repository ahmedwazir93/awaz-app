    Off-plan
    Buying a property before it's built, usually on a payment plan.
    
          
    EOI
    Expression of interest. A refundable deposit that holds your place in the queue before a launch.
    
          
    SPA
    Sale and purchase agreement: the contract between you and the seller or developer.
    
          
    Oqood
    Dubai's register for off-plan sales. Your SPA is recorded here until handover.
    
          
    Escrow
    A project-only bank account. Off-plan payments go here, not to the developer.
    
          
    Payment plan
    How the price is split, e.g. 20/40/40: booking, during construction, at handover.
    
          
    Post-handover plan
    Part of the price paid after you get the keys, often over 2 to 5 years.
    
          
    Handover
    When the building is finished and you take possession.
    
          
    Title deed
    The certificate proving you own the property, issued by the land department.
    
          
    NOC
    No-objection certificate from the developer, needed to resell. Capped at AED 5,000 for off-plan in Dubai.
    
          
    DLD
    Dubai Land Department. Registers every property deal and collects the 4% transfer fee.
    
          
    RERA
    Dubai's real estate regulator. Licenses brokers and supervises developers and escrow.
    
          
    BRN
    Broker registration number. Every licensed Dubai broker has one. Mine is 85446.
    
          
    Ejari
    Dubai's lease register. A tenancy isn't valid until it's on Ejari.
    
          
    Service charge
    Yearly fee for maintaining a building's shared areas, charged per sq ft (or sq m). In Dubai, balconies and terraces count at 25%.
    
          
    Freehold
    Full ownership of the property and land, open to foreign buyers in designated areas.
    
          
    Musataha
    A long-term right to build on and use someone else's land, common in Abu Dhabi.
    
          
    LTV
    Loan to value: how much of the price a bank will lend, e.g. 80% on a first ready home.
    
          
    Gross yield
    Yearly rent divided by the price, before costs.
    
          
    Golden Visa
    A 10-year renewable UAE residence visa, open to property owners at AED 2M or more.
    
        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    
          
    Can foreigners buy property in the UAE?
    Yes. Foreign buyers can own freehold in designated areas of every emirate, which covers most of the communities investors buy in. You don't need to live here.
    
          
    What does it cost to buy, on top of the price?
    Mainly the transfer fee: 4% in Dubai and most emirates, 2% in Abu Dhabi. Add admin fees, and an agency fee on resales. Use the total cost tab above for your number.
    
          
    Is off-plan safe?
    In Dubai, your payments go into an escrow account the developer can't touch, and projects must be registered before they're sold. Check the developer's track record too.
    
          
    How much deposit do I need for off-plan?
    Usually 10% to 20% at booking, plus the transfer fee. The rest follows the payment plan.
    
          
    Can I get a mortgage as a foreigner?
    Yes. UAE residents can borrow up to 80% on a first ready home under AED 5M. Non-residents and off-plan purchases are usually capped at 50%.
    
          
    Does buying property give me a visa?
    Property worth AED 2M or more can qualify you for a 10-year Golden Visa. Dubai and Abu Dhabi count it differently, so see the Golden Visa guide.
    
          
    Can I sell before handover?
    Usually, once you've paid the developer's minimum (often 30% to 40%) and got their NOC. Use the selling before handover tab to check your profit.
    
          
    Is there tax on rental income?
    The UAE has no personal income tax on rent and no capital gains tax. Your home country may tax it, as India does for residents.
    
          
    Can I buy without visiting?
    Yes. Contracts can be signed digitally and handover done through a power of attorney. See the buying from abroad guide.
    
          
    How do I check a broker is licensed?
    Every Dubai broker has a BRN you can check in the Dubai REST app. Mine is 85446.

    Who's who in Dubai real estate
    
      
    The Dubai Land Department (DLD) sits at the centre. It registers every sale, mortgage and lease in Dubai (except inside DIFC, which has its own registry and courts). Its regulatory arm, RERA, licenses and supervises brokers, developers, escrow accounts and building management.
    
      

      
    Buying off-plan, step by step
    
      
    Off-plan sales are governed by Law No. 13 of 2008 on the Interim Property Register (Oqood), as amended. A developer can't sell a single unit until the plot is theirs and the project is registered.
    
      
      
      
        
    • Developers can't charge a transfer fee when you buy or resell. They can charge for a no-objection certificate (NOC), capped at AED 5,000 for off-plan.
      • 
        
    • Foreign buyers can own freehold in the areas designated under Regulation No. 3 of 2006, which covers most of the communities investors buy in.
      • 
      
    

      
    Where your off-plan money goes
    
      
    Law No. 8 of 2007 is the investor's main protection. Your payments never go straight to the developer.
    
      

      
    If someone doesn't pay, or doesn't deliver
    
      
    If a buyer misses payments, the developer can't just cancel. It has to notify DLD, which gives the buyer 30 days to catch up. After that, what the developer may keep depends on how far construction has gone (Law No. 19 of 2020).
    
      
      
    When the buyer can walk away. You can ask the court to cancel your SPA and refund you if the developer:
    
      
      
        
    • refuses without good reason to give you a final sale contract;
      • 
        
    • doesn't tie the payment schedule to the approved construction schedule;
      • 
        
    • materially changes your unit on its own, for example a sea view becomes a park view; or
      • 
        
    • hands over a unit that can't be used because of a major structural defect.
      •

    Who's who in Abu Dhabi real estate
    
      
    The Abu Dhabi Real Estate Centre (ADREC) regulates the sector. It sits under the Department of Municipalities and Transport (DMT) and took over the real estate functions of the old municipal registration departments.
    
      

      
    Owning and registering property
    
      
    
        
    Register or it doesn't countOwnership passes only on registration. An unregistered deal binds the two parties personally but gives no rights against anyone else.
    
        
    Who can ownUAE nationals can own residential, commercial, investment and agricultural land. Foreign buyers own in designated investment zones.
    
        
    Long leasesA lease of more than 4 years must be registered. Shorter leases can be noted on the register if you ask.
    
        
    Valuation checkIf the agreed price is 20% or more away from the official index, the seller needs a valuation certificate. Fees are charged on whichever is higher.
    
        
    Mortgage prioritySeveral mortgages on one property rank in the order they were registered.
    
        
    Free transfersInheritance and the first registration of an allocated plot are exempt from fees.
    
      
    

      
    How off-plan money is protected
    
      
    Every off-plan project has its own escrow account. Under the 2025 amendment, the money in it can only go on building the project and repaying project finance.
    
      

      
    If an off-plan buyer stops paying
    
      
    Law No. 2 of 2025 sets out a fixed process. The developer can cancel without going to court, but only after these steps.
    
      

      
    Living in a building: owners' committees and service fees
    
      
    The 2025 law replaced owners' associations and their boards with owners' committees formed by ADREC. Decision No. 25 of 2025 sets the day-to-day rules.
    
      

      
    Government fees
    
      
    
        
    Transfer feeSet between 1% and 4% of the price, and 2% in practice (ADM). Al Reem and Al Maryah are under ADGM, also 2%.
    
        
    Mortgage0.1% of the loan, or 0.05% for housing loans, capped at AED 1M per transaction. AED 200 to transfer a mortgage.

    Gifts & willsGift to close relatives: AED 3,000 (residential) or AED 10,000 (commercial). Registering a will: AED 3,000.
    
      
    

      
    Licences and penalties
    
      
    No one can work in Abu Dhabi real estate without an ADREC licence. That covers developers, brokers, brokers' employees, auctioneers, valuers and surveyors.

    UAENo income tax on rent and no capital gains tax. The one-off 4% DLD transfer fee applies in Dubai.

    Quick answers
    
      
    Can Americans buy property in Dubai?
    Yes. Freehold areas are open to all nationalities, and you don't need a UAE visa to buy.
    Do I pay US tax on my Dubai rent?
    Yes, as a US citizen or resident, wherever you live. You can deduct costs and depreciation.
    Do I report the property on FBAR?
    No, not property held directly in your name. Your UAE bank accounts are reportable if they total over $10K at any point in the year.
    Can I get a mortgage?
    US lenders won't lend on UAE property. UAE banks lend to non-residents, usually around 50%, and to UAE residents up to 80% on a first home.
